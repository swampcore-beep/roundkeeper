System Reference Document 5.2.1
-------------------------------
This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.
Used for: the list of spells (names, levels, Concentration, duration, class lists) and the class resources with their recharge rules, and the built-in game data in srd/srd-5.2.1.json: monsters and their statblocks, spells, magic items and equipment, conditions and rules.

The structured text of srd/srd-5.2.1.json comes from Open5e's transcription of the SRD (https://github.com/open5e/open5e-api, data/v2/wizards-of-the-coast/srd-2024). Open5e's own license (a modified MIT license, Copyright (c) 2018 Ean Moody) leaves out the SRD content provided by third parties; that content is Wizards of the Coast's, under CC BY 4.0 as stated above. Roundkeeper converted it into the homebrew JSON format with tools/srd_from_open5e.py; the changes are formatting only (fields renamed, lists and tables restructured, "Hit:" restored after attack ranges, condition names marked).
